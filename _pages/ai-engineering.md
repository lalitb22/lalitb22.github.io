  <h2 id="learning-method">How to use this journal</h2>
  <p class="section-description">Learn to run models, validate their responses, retrieve relevant context, connect tools, and evaluate results. Each chapter develops a skill you can reuse across different applications.</p>
  <p class="section-description">Spend the first hour understanding and reproducing the baseline, the second changing one variable at a time, and the third recording results and failure cases. Keep prompts, settings, model versions, and measurements together so comparisons remain useful.</p>
  <p class="section-description">Day 1 documents local inference experiments with <code>qwen2.5vl:3b</code>. Day 2 develops validated, typed outputs with Ollama and Pydantic, distinguishing tested extraction results from unverified retry recovery. These are observations from specific runs; hardware, input length, caching, and runtime settings can change the results. Further tutorials will be linked as they are published.</p>
</section>
