I'm Lalit, a software engineer and systems builder based in India.

**Lets_Writebout_It** is a journal of practical experiments in systems, AI models, and architecture. The focus is on understanding what happens when an idea meets a working system: what works, how it behaves, and where it needs improvement.

## What you'll find here

- Engineering notes on AI models, local inference, and practical workflows.
- Technical articles on experiments, tools, and architecture.
- A [10-day AI Engineering roadmap]({{ '/ai-engineering/' | relative_url }}) with chapters linked as they are published.
